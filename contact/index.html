Contact Happy Valley 24/7 Plumbing

The fastest way to reach us is by phone, any hour of any day. This is a phone-first business, so please call instead of sending a message when something is wrong.

For emergencies and scheduling, call (503) 974-3329. The line is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including nights, weekends and holidays. If you smell gas, leave the home and call 911 or your gas utility before you call us.

What to have ready when you call

The more you can tell us, the faster the plumber can help. A short list helps:

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • What is happening, such as leaking, backed up, no hot water or low pressure.
  • Whether you have closed the main water valve.
  • Gas or electric water heater, and its approximate age.
  • Any photos you can take of the problem.

Email

Email is for non-urgent questions and paperwork at contact@247plumberhappyvalley.com. We cannot promise that an email will be seen in time for an emergency, so please call for anything that cannot wait.
