The first question in a newer home: who covers it

In a home that is only a few years old, a plumbing problem may fall under a builder warranty, a manufacturer warranty or neither. Sort that out before anyone opens a wall. Calling a plumber does not void anything by itself, but unapproved repairs on a covered item can.

  • Find your closing paperwork and warranty booklet. Note the dates and what is covered.
  • Check whether the builder wants to be notified before any repair.
  • Register fixtures and the water heater with their manufacturers if you have not.
  • Take dated photos of the problem for any claim.

We are glad to inspect and give a clear price, and we will tell you if it looks like something the builder should see first.

Either one benefits from the maker's maintenance steps, such as flushing a tank or descaling a tankless unit on the schedule in its manual. Leaks and pressure in the first years

Hidden leaks in newer homes are not more common than elsewhere, but they can go unseen behind finished walls. Watch for stains, a hissing sound or a water bill that rises with no change in use. Read the meter with everything off to see if it moves. Our leak detection page explains how a plumber finds the source without cutting more than needed.

Drains in a home with finished landscaping

Newer lots often have young trees and recent landscaping. Slow drains are usually grease, hair or wipes, not roots. For a single slow fixture, a plunger or a drain cleaner visit is enough. For several backed-up fixtures, call, because that points to the main line, and a camera look on the sewer inspection page shows what is going on. Avoid putting wipes labeled flushable down any toilet.

Before you call

  • Locate the main shut-off and close it if water is flowing.
  • Have your builder warranty paperwork and closing date nearby.
  • Take photos and note when you first noticed the problem.
  • Identify whether it is one fixture or several.
  • Check the water heater label for the model and manufacture date.

Code and permits
