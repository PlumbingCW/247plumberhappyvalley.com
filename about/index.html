About Happy Valley 24/7 Plumbing

A phone-first plumbing business based on SE Sunnyside Road in Happy Valley, built around one idea: when plumbing fails, you should reach someone any hour of the day.

Call anytime, 24/7(503) 974-3329Call now

Happy Valley 24/7 Plumbing is based at 17155 SE Sunnyside Rd in Happy Valley, Oregon. We handle emergency and planned plumbing for homes and businesses in Happy Valley, Clackamas, Damascus, Milwaukie and Oregon City. The business is phone-first by design. There are no booking forms on this site, because a plumbing emergency does not wait for an email reply.

How a call works

You call (503) 974-3329 at any hour. Someone asks what is happening, where you are and whether water is still flowing, then tells you what to do right away, such as closing the main valve. A plumber is scheduled, and you are given an arrival window.

When the plumber arrives, they find the cause, explain the options and give you a clear price before any work begins. If the job turns out larger than expected, they stop and review it with you first.

Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows. We do not promise a specific arrival minute, because traffic, earlier emergencies and the nature of the job all affect timing. We do promise to tell you honestly what to expect.

Licensing in Oregon, and how to check it yourself

Oregon licenses plumbers through the Building Codes Division, and plumbing businesses work under licensing from the state's Construction Contractors Board or employ people who do. Both agencies run public online license searches. You can look up any plumber or contractor by name or license number before they start work, and you are welcome to do that with us.

Our plumbers are licensed and insured, and we are happy to point you to the lookup tools if you want to confirm it.

We do not list license numbers or insurance details on this site, because they belong to specific people and policies, and we would rather you check the official records than take a web page's word for it.

What we handle

Most calls fall into a few groups. Drains and sewer lines include drain cleaning, hydro jetting and sewer inspection. Water heaters include repair, replacement and tankless installs. Leaks run from a dripping faucet to a burst pipe, with leak detection for water that cannot be found. We also do repiping and light commercial plumbing.

Why Happy Valley homes have their own needs

The city grew fast, from roughly 1,500 housing units in 2000 to more than 8,000 by 2020, and much of that housing sits on the slopes of Mount Scott and Scouters Mountain.

Newer homes usually have PEX supply lines and PVC drains, which fail in different ways than the cast iron and galvanized steel found in older Milwaukie and Oregon City houses. Hillside lots also mean higher water pressure in some neighborhoods, which is hard on water heaters and fixtures.

Knowing this helps the plumber arrive with the right parts and ask better questions on the phone.

How we explain prices

Prices depend on the fixture, the access, the parts and the time of day. We do not quote a number sight unseen for a job that needs diagnosis. Once the plumber has found the cause, you hear the price for the work in front of you, and you decide whether to go ahead.

If you want to compare, you can say so, and the plumber will leave a clear written scope you can show someone else.

Standards we work to

Oregon plumbing work follows the Oregon Plumbing Specialty Code, administered locally through building departments such as the City of Happy Valley's Building Division. Permit rules vary by project, and we tell you when a job is likely to need one so you can confirm with the building department. We pressure test lines, set water heaters with relief valves, drain pans and bracing, and leave work areas clean.

What to expect from the plumber

The plumber introduces themselves, confirms the problem and shuts off water if it is still running. They protect floors where they work, explain what they find in plain words and show you the failed part when there is one. When the work is done, they test it, clean up and tell you what to watch for over the next few days.

What we will not do

We will not pressure you into a replacement when a repair is sound. We will not hide extra charges, because the price is explained before work starts. We will not claim to handle work outside plumbing, such as drywall repair or mold removal, and we say so when a job needs another trade.
