How to choose between similar services

Several services overlap, and picking the right one saves time. A clog in one fixture is a drain cleaning job, while several backed-up drains point to sewer line cleaning. A heater that is leaking from the tank needs replacement, but one with a failed part needs repair. Unexplained water use belongs under leak detection, and a pipe that is actively spraying is burst pipe repair.

If you are still unsure, call. The person who answers will ask a few questions, tell you what to do right away and schedule the right plumber.
