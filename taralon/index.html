Taralon is named in the City of Happy Valley's own planning material as the Taralon Master Planned Community, with a community center, open spaces and a trail system provided by the developer. Homes on SE Taralon Dr and nearby streets use ZIP code 97015, and many carry a Clackamas mailing name. The city, not the mailing name, tells you where you live.

A master planned community usually has a homeowner association. That is the main thing that makes plumbing here different from a street of unrelated houses, and it is the focus of this page.

Water and sewer

Sunrise Water Authority or Clackamas River Water supplies the water depending on the address. Water Environment Services handles sewer. Check your bill.

Check the association before exterior work

Many associations regulate what can change outside the home. A plumber working inside a wall rarely needs permission, but exterior work can. Replacing a water heater in a garage seldom triggers review, while trenching a yard for a water line or sewer repair may. Read your covenants, ask the board when unsure and keep any approval in writing before the plumber digs.

  • Interior fixture, faucet and toilet repairs: usually no association step.
  • Water heater replacement: confirm any rules about venting or exterior changes.
  • Yard excavation for a line repair: ask about landscaping, irrigation and approvals.
  • Shared common areas: report to the association, not a plumber.

Private lines, public lines and common areas

In a planned community, some pipes serve one house and others serve many. The line from the house to the sewer connection is usually the owner's, while the public main belongs to the sewer agency. Common-area irrigation and amenity plumbing around the community center belong to the association. If you see a leak in a common area, tell the association.

If it is on your lot, call us. Our sewer inspection page explains how a camera shows where a line problem is.

Everyday calls

Most calls from homes like these are for slow drains, water heaters that stop heating or are leaking, running toilets and leaks that show as stains or a rising water bill. A drain cleaning visit handles a slow fixture, and a water heater repair visit starts with the label so the plumber knows the model and age. If you have a leak you cannot find, see leak detection.

We do not assume the age or material of your pipes. The plumber identifies them on site and explains the options.

Before you call

  • Close the main shut-off valve if water is running.
  • Note the street address and the problem.
  • Take photos of any damage or error codes.
  • Check association rules if work will be outside the home.
  • Tell us if the water heater is in the garage or an attic.

Code and permits

Oregon plumbing follows the Oregon Plumbing Specialty Code, based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). Permit rules vary, so ask the Happy Valley building department before replacing a water heater or changing pipe layout.
